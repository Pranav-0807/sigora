# SIGORA Final Experience Update — 1 Sep 2026

## Included
- Focus navigation drawer (feature menu hidden until requested).
- English, Tamil and Hindi product-language framework, expanded across core navigation, dashboard, settings, account/import experiences and common UI labels.
- SIGORA Intelligence surfaced as the forward-looking dashboard experience.
- Statement-linked Bank Accounts: users can add a bank/account without credentials and import statements into that account.
- Universal statement normalization for common machine-readable PDF, CSV, TSV, TXT, XLSX, XLS, OFX and QFX exports.
- Duplicate protection and normalized SIGORA transaction fields.
- Existing Setu Account Aggregator routes preserved for future consent-based live sync.

## Safety / honesty
- SIGORA never requests bank passwords, OTPs, card PINs or UPI PINs.
- Statement import is not advertised as live bank syncing.
- Image-only/scanned PDFs may require a machine-readable export; SIGORA does not pretend OCR is reliable financial ingestion.
- User-entered merchant descriptions, goal names, tickers and financial records are not translated.
- Legal/financial disclaimers retain source wording where a reviewed translation is not explicitly provided.
