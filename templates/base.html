How Sigora works

From your first transaction to a clearer view of your money.

01

Track your money

Add transactions manually or import a statement. Sigora turns the activity you record into the foundation for your dashboard and reports.

Transactions → Import → Dashboard
02

Give your money a plan

Create monthly budgets and savings goals, then keep them updated as your spending and progress change.

Budgets → Goals
03

Understand the bigger picture

Reports, Financial Health Score, forecasts and budget-risk signals help connect everyday activity to longer-term patterns.

Reports → Intelligence
04

Research your investments

Track holdings, build a watchlist and open market charts with plain-language context. Market information is educational and may be delayed.

Invest → Watchlist → Market charts
05

Use SIGORA AI thoughtfully

Insights can explain patterns in the financial data you record. They are educational, can be imperfect, and are not personalized investment advice.

Insights → Intelligence → What-if
06

You stay in control

Manage your profile, alerts and account from Settings. Premium uses secure Razorpay checkout when available for your billing currency. Never share passwords, OTPs or banking credentials with Sigora support.

Settings → Premium → Support
